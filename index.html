Frequently asked questions

How long does a private ADHD assessment take?
Most private ADHD assessments involve one or two appointments — typically a detailed clinical interview followed by structured diagnostic testing — and are usually completed within 2 to 4 weeks from your first enquiry, compared to NHS waits that can now exceed 7 years in some areas.
What happens after diagnosis?
If you receive an ADHD diagnosis, your assessor will provide a full written report outlining the findings and recommended next steps, which may include therapy, workplace or school adjustments, or a referral for a discussion about medication with a prescriber.
Do I need a GP referral?
No. Private ADHD assessments are almost always self-referral, meaning you can book directly without seeing your GP first. You're welcome to speak to your GP too, but it isn't a requirement to get started.
Is a private diagnosis accepted by schools and employers?
Yes. A diagnosis from a properly accredited, HCPC registered assessor carries the same clinical validity as an NHS diagnosis and is widely accepted by schools, universities, and employers for arranging reasonable adjustments and support.
How much does a private ADHD assessment cost?
Costs vary by provider and by the complexity of the assessment, but private ADHD assessments in the UK typically range from around £600 to £1,500 for the full diagnostic process. We'll always be upfront about pricing before you commit to anything.
