NHS physiotherapy waiting times in Greater Manchester — the data

Published 26 January 2026 · NHS waiting lists

Waiting times for NHS musculoskeletal (MSK) physiotherapy vary significantly across Greater Manchester's boroughs, but the overall picture is consistent: demand is outstripping capacity, and patients are waiting longer than clinical guidelines recommend to start treatment for injuries and chronic pain.

The headline number

At any given time, an estimated 16,124 patients are waiting for NHS MSK physiotherapy across Greater Manchester. That figure covers everything from post-surgical rehabilitation to sports injuries to long-term back and joint pain — conditions where early treatment is strongly linked to a faster, fuller recovery.

Why waiting matters clinically

Physiotherapy is time-sensitive in a way that's easy to underestimate. A minor strain treated within a week or two often resolves quickly. The same injury, left untreated for two or three months while waiting for an appointment, can develop compensatory movement patterns, muscle weakness, or chronic pain that takes considerably longer to treat. Waiting lists don't just delay treatment — they can make the underlying problem worse.

Variation across boroughs

Waiting times differ between Manchester, Stockport, Trafford, Salford, and the other Greater Manchester boroughs, driven by local commissioning decisions and clinic capacity. Wherever you live in the region, though, the direction of travel over recent years has been the same: longer waits, not shorter ones.

What are the alternatives?

For many people, private physiotherapy is now the realistic route to being seen quickly — often within 48 hours rather than several weeks — without needing a GP referral. It isn't free, but for time-sensitive injuries, the cost is frequently weighed against the risk of a minor issue becoming a much bigger one.
