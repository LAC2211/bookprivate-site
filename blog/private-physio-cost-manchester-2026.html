How much does private physiotherapy cost in Manchester in 2026?

Published 12 January 2026 · Guide

If you've been quoted an 18-week wait for NHS physiotherapy in Greater Manchester, you've probably already started looking at private options — and probably found prices that seem to vary wildly from clinic to clinic. Here's a realistic breakdown of what private physiotherapy actually costs in Manchester in 2026, and what you're paying for at each price point.

Typical price ranges

A standard initial assessment with a private physiotherapist in and around Manchester typically costs between £45 and £75. Follow-up sessions are usually cheaper, in the £35 to £60 range, and most people need somewhere between three and six sessions depending on the injury. That puts a full course of treatment for a common issue — a bad back, a knee niggle, a shoulder impingement — at roughly £150 to £400 in total.

What affects the price

Location matters: clinics in central Manchester and affluent suburbs like Altrincham or Didsbury tend to sit at the higher end, while clinics further out can be noticeably cheaper without any drop in quality. Practitioner experience matters too — a senior physiotherapist with 15 years treating sports injuries will usually charge more than someone newly qualified, though newly qualified doesn't mean worse, just different.

Is it worth it compared to waiting for the NHS?

This is really a question about the cost of time. NHS physiotherapy is free at the point of use, but the average wait in Greater Manchester currently runs into many weeks — and a lot can happen to an injury while you wait, including it becoming a longer-term chronic problem that takes more sessions to fix. Most people who go private are paying for early intervention, not just convenience.
